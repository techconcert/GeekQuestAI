What is new in GeekQuestAI r4:

- Elevator: when a floor of 30 racks is full he builds another floor (up to 4 floors, 120 racks) and rides up to fill it with servers and upgrade the hardware.
- The solar farm moved outside: a fenced field of 30 panels per screen, up to 4 screens linked by gates. Crates of panels keep extending it.
- Little cleaner robots (bought at the hardware store, up to 3 per screen) patrol the farm and wipe dirty panels.
- Power and cooling are now resources, not just numbers: solar panels give power, cooling towers (up to 7) give cooling. If either is below what the racks need, TLOPS is throttled.
- The top bar alternates between TLOPS and PWR / H2O bars with the percentages.
- The hardware store also sells cooling towers and cleaner robots.
- Smaller save record (the fresh game starts from scratch: a game saved by r1 to r3 is not carried over). Update and recovery pages unchanged.
- Browser preview: floors, farm screens, towers, cleaners and the power and cooling percentages.
